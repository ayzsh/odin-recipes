One Pan Salt and Vinegar Chicken and Potatoes

Desciption:

This one-pan chicken and potatoes recipe brings the tangy, savory taste of everyone’s favorite salt and vinegar chips to a hearty skillet dinner. Juicy, golden-brown chicken thighs and tender baby potatoes soak up bold flavor from salt and vinegar seasoning, white vinegar, and Dijon mustard. Ready in about an hour, it’s a fun twist on a classic dinner with easy cleanup.

Ingredients:

  • 2 tablespoons salt and vinegar seasoning, divided
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 4 skin-on, bone-in chicken thighs (1 1/2 to 2 pounds)
  • 3 tablespoon olive oil, divided
  • 1 medium onion, thinly sliced (1 cup)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup white vinegar
  • 3/4 cup chicken broth, divided
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 1/2 pounds baby potatoes, halved
  • 2 tablespoons butter, cut up
  • Chopped green onions and/or fresh parsley, for garnish

Steps:

  1. Combine 1 1/2 teaspoons salt and vinegar seasoning and pepper in a small bowl. Pat chicken thighs dry with paper towels and season both sides with spice mixture.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in an extra-large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Place chicken thighs in skillet skin-side down. Cook until browned, 4 to 5 minutes each side. Transfer to a plate.
  3. Add onions to skillet. Cook over medium, stirring occasionally, until tender, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and 1 1/2 teaspoons salt and vinegar seasoning; stir and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add vinegar and cook until mostly evaporated, about 4 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile in a small bowl, whisk together chicken broth, mustard, remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil and 1 tablespoon salt and vinegar seasoning. Pour into skillet. Return to a boil. Add potatoes and stir to coat. Reduce heat, cover and cook 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, adding 2 tablespoons of chicken broth halfway through.
  5. Nestle chicken thighs into the potatoes. Scatter butter over the top and drizzle with the remaining 2 tablespoons chicken broth. Cover and cook over medium-low heat until potatoes are tender and chicken is no longer pink at the center, 10 to 14 minutes. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the chicken, not touching the bone, should register at least 165 degrees F (80 degrees C).
  6. Garnish with chopped green onions and/or parsley.
